## FAC-02 — Party

**FAC-D01 approved:** Introduce a bounded Domain responsibility `party` for tenant-scoped real-world natural-person and organisational counterparty references, including parties without accounts. Identity owns principals/groups/tenants; Profile owns descriptive personal facts under its existing authority; Verification owns qualified attestations and trust findings; legal registries remain authoritative for external registered facts. Party owns local party identity, qualified legal form/name/identifier and address/contact references, source provenance, effective dates, relationship to tenant/principal/profile references, duplicate/merge/split/correction lifecycle, permitted representation evidence references and disclosure. A party record is not proof of legal existence, authority, tax status or access. No global person matching or cross-tenant disclosure; account linkage is optional and independently authorised. Existing Profile facts are referenced, not reowned. Where a non-account party's necessary contact data has no existing owner, Party may retain strictly minimised purpose-bound contact facts subject to Privacy and legal obligations. Domain roles (seller, buyer, employer, payer, beneficiary) remain source-owned relationship facts; Party may supply scoped directory labels but never grant those roles.

**Alternatives:** Identity extension would conflate digital standing and legal status; Profile extension would conflate personal descriptors and organisational/counterparty identity; Verification extension would turn evidence into a master; separate customer/supplier registries would duplicate identity. A dedicated Party boundary is justified across Order, Billing, Invoice, Accounting, Subscription, Employment, Recruitment, Membership and Payment Gateway. No mandatory new package.

## FAC-03 — Payroll

**FAC-D02 approved detailed scope; independent boundary already approved EMP-02.** Payroll owns versioned pay-run calculation and approval, gross/net earnings, qualified deductions/contributions, employer liabilities, adjustments, pay statements, filing and disbursement instructions and qualified receipts/reconciliation. Employment owns engagement, approved contractual pay/leave facts; Time & Attendance owns approved actual hours; Legal Compliance supplies qualified jurisdiction rules; Accounting owns ledger and settlement recognition; external disbursement/provider authority remains separate. No generic bank transfer is presumed supported by Payment Gateway. Support native calculation only for explicitly verified jurisdiction/rule coverage; an external payroll authority may be selected per employer/pay regime and effective period. Unsupported tax/legal regimes fail closed, not approximate. Native manual evidence intake may preserve meaningful operation without claiming statutory calculation.

## FAC-04 — Workforce Scheduling

**FAC-D03 approved detailed scope; planned/actual split already approved EMP-03.** Workforce Scheduling owns planned shifts, published rosters, assignment offers/acceptance, availability declarations for rostering, coverage, conflicts, revisions, approved changes and withdrawal. Employment owns engagement, contractual working patterns and leave entitlement; Time & Attendance owns actual observations and approved statements; Qualification/Verification supply evidence; Facility/Booking own spaces/capacity/reservations; Scheduler executes technical triggers only. Do not infer attendance, pay, assignment authority or legal compliance from a published shift. Support manual rostering first, optional bounded optimisation later, with explainable constraints and human approval. No separate calendar master.

## FAC-05 — Budget and establishment

**FAC-D04 approved:** Accounting owns financial budget baselines and governed allocation/commitment-control evidence for its book/legal-entity scope, without owning source orders or employment. Project retains its project plan/target and may request a separate approved financial allocation. Distinguish planned, approved, reserved, committed, recognised and settled; do not claim a reservation without authoritative confirmation. Employment owns approved workforce establishment allocations by employer, organisational scope, position definition/version, capacity unit (headcount/FTE), effective period and approved financial mandate reference. Job owns reusable position definitions; Recruitment owns approved recruitment openings and selection; Employment owns actual occupancy. Staffing authorisation does not create employment or reserve money. Reserve/release capacity atomically at its owner; external authoritative finance/HR provider modes are qualified. No separate Finance or establishment capability initially. These are approved conceptual extensions to existing owners, not implemented interfaces.

## FAC-07 — Commercial agreements and procurement

**FAC-D05 approved:** Order owns sales/purchase commitments, approved purchase requests that mature into orders, amendments and fulfilment/return obligations; do not invent an all-purpose procurement owner. Subscription owns recurring service/entitlement agreements; Employment and Membership retain their specialist agreements. Publisher owns document versions and publication, not executable contractual obligations. Party owns counterparty identity, not agreements. Billing owns rating and charging arrangements; Accounting owns financial recognition. Under approved FAC-D06, Order owns purchase/sale framework terms; other general agreements retain qualified external authority until a justified independent native lifecycle is approved. Assess whether Order can own the relevant purchase/sale framework terms and whether other agreement types have a demonstrated independent lifecycle before authorising a new Contract Management capability. Until that gate is resolved, use qualified external agreement references and versioned accepted term snapshots supplied by the responsible host/domain; never claim a local master or infer assent. No automatic new Procurement, CRM or Contracts artefact.

## Residual agreement assessment — FAC-D05

Order can own purchase/sale framework terms directly related to its commercial commitments: versioned supplier/buyer references, approved price/quantity/validity terms, call-off limits, amendment and cancellation, linkage to resulting orders. Subscription, Employment and Membership retain their specialist agreements. Other agreements (leases, licences, service-level undertakings, financing, partnership or settlement instruments) differ materially in authority and lifecycle; a common document is not a common executable obligation. Asset, Facility, Accounting, Legal Compliance and other source owners may consume independently evidenced terms but do not acquire general agreement authority by doing so. Publisher may retain the document, Workflow approval evidence, Party counterparty evidence; none proves contractual assent.

**FAC-D06 approved:** extend Order only for commercial purchase/sale framework terms and retain externally owned general agreements as qualified references until a demonstrated cross-domain native lifecycle warrants an independent Contracts capability. Require explicit authority designation, accepted immutable term revision, parties/representation, scope/effective period, provenance, amendments and expiry/withdrawal. No invented local agreement master. For unsupported native agreement management, fail closed or use the designated external authority; no silent transfer of obligations. This is a deliberate scope boundary, not a claim that every general agreement requirement has been implemented. Review again when lease/licensing/financing or multi-domain obligation-management requirements justify it.
