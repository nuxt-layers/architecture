## Purpose and boundary

Moderation provides the **independent cross-domain case, investigation, decision, appeal and enforcement-reconciliation lifecycle** for reported content or conduct. It owns cases, not the underlying comments, documents, images, listings, groups, scoreboards or remote Node data. Content Security owns detector verdicts; Legal Compliance owns legally qualified duties; Privacy owns processing constraints; Audit owns accepted evidence custody. Source capabilities own their resource state and actually enforce or reverse restrictions. A source-native emergency hold must never depend on Moderation being available.

Safeguarding and identity/age/representation evidence are purpose-limited claims from their authoritative providers; Moderation may use them in a case but does not prove identity or age. Gaming is a catalogue/launch/accepted-score portal, **not a gameplay engine**; individual game layers retain gameplay and original results. Course owns learning content and withdrawal, not moderation findings or learner grades. Federation owns peer exchange; Search owns derived indexes; Hub is an application composition, not a universal content owner. Localisation supplies translation and language-quality signals but never lifts a safety restriction; translated case explanations remain privacy-scoped. Media's currently approved scope includes images, PDF and limited text/CSV/Markdown resources; each applicable source keeps type- and revision-specific content safeguards.

## Classification, authority and native operation

**Classification: Platform (approved MOD-D6).** Reports and cases are reusable across unrelated resource-owning domains and retain independent evidence, appeal and disposition lifecycles. This supports an independently owned bounded capability, but **not** a mandatory separate service, Nuxt Layer or database server: a modular monolith with a headless core is the default. Moderation is optional to applications with no case-management needs, and absence cannot disable source-owned emergency holds and their own legal/security controls.

**Authoritative case facts:** case ID/tenant/initiator, classified report source and safe reporter follow-up, joined source references and revisions, triage/severity, policy/jurisdiction snapshots, qualified investigation and decision with reasons, conflict-of-interest assignments, appeal submission and independent outcome, action-intent ledger, per-target enforcement receipts and reconciliation, deadlines, lawful disclosure/referral approvals, case closure/reopening, retention/holds and case-owned durable outbox. Evidence bytes and original publication remain with their owners or explicitly approved restricted evidence custodian. Audit owns evidential custody of accepted events, not Moderation's case truth.

**Native lifecycle:** received/validated → triaged (or declined with safe rationale) → investigating → decision pending → decided → action requested → per-target confirmed, refused, failed or unknown → notifications/appeal/reconsideration → closed or reopened. Investigation, enforcement, appeal and statutory handoff are **orthogonal state tracks**, not one lossy global enum. Protect urgent cases with due dates and manual escalation; do not conflate a report, scanner flag, moderation finding, criminal conviction or completed takedown. On reversal, preserve prior historical outcomes and initiate separately reauthorised owner-state restoration; appeal does not guarantee reinstatement if other holds apply.

**Native without optional providers:** accept safe reports, conduct authorised human triage/investigation, record decisions, coordinate direct owner ports where configured, admit appeals and reconcile acknowledgements. No automatic trusted detection or statutory filing is invented. Mandatory IAM checks, qualified legal rules or persistence, if required by the selected operation, fail closed; unavailable scanning/Workflow/Scheduler/Notifications cannot be silently treated as success. Local source protection continues if Moderation is down.

## Idealised public outbound contract

| Surface / permitted consumer | Authoritative meaning and limitations |
|---|---|
| **MOD-O1 report receipt** — reporter/host | Opaque case reference or safe tracking token, accepted/duplicate/rejected status and allowed next action; does not reveal target visibility, other reporters or investigation |
| **MOD-O2 case query/queue** — authorised moderator, supervisor, operator | Tenant/role-filtered case summary, subject-safe evidence references, assignments, deadlines, policy/evidence version, completeness/freshness and redacted view; denies cross-case fishing |
| **MOD-O3 adjudication result** — affected parties and authorised source owners | Reason-coded, policy-versioned decision, exact scope/target revision, deciding authority, time, reviewability and disclosure-approved explanation. **Not proof of executed restriction** |
| **MOD-O4 action intent** — selected source owner via public port | Scoped request ID and idempotency, exact tenant/target/revision, action, urgency, expiry, reason category, authority and case correlation; recipient must independently reauthorise, may reject |
| **MOD-O5 enforcement reconciliation** — operator, affected owner, case reporting | Per-target requested/accepted/completed/refused/failed/unknown status, receipt provenance and effective time, outstanding retries/compensation; never silently aggregate partial completion to full success |
| **MOD-O6 appeal and correction** — eligible appellant, independent reviewer, source owner | Appeal acceptance and decision, changed evidence/decision revision, reasoned outcome and separately requested reversal, preserving original history and applicable competing holds |
| **MOD-O7 legal handoff** — explicitly designated filing/disclosure process | Approved, minimised case fact package, applicable duty/effective version, disclosure scope, authorised sign-off and handoff reference; transport acknowledgment and statutory acceptance separately tracked |
| **MOD-O8 evidence/audit event** — Audit and protected authorised evidence consumers | Attributable minimal case transitions, access, decisions, disclosures, holds and enforcement discrepancies with sensitivity classification; immutable Audit receipt remains Audit-owned |
| **MOD-O9 committed lifecycle events** — notifications, reporting, domain subscribers, Message Queue transport | CaseOpened, CaseTriaged, DecisionRecorded, ActionRequested, EnforcementReceiptRecorded, AppealResolved, CaseClosed/Reopened and DisclosureApproved (illustrative names). Committed facts only, with tenant scope, event ID, sequence/version, correlation, lawful disclosure classification |
| **MOD-O10 operator service state** — authorised host GUI/Observability | Queue/backlog, escalation, evidence-gap and receipt-lag indicators with denominators and freshness; health does not imply legal compliance; no raw sensitive evidence in telemetry |

**Command/query/event invariant:** Commands validate trusted caller, owner scope, policy and expected revision, return stable denial/conflict/unknown codes, and deduplicate by scoped intent key. Queries enforce purpose-based read privileges, redaction and freshness and do not leak the existence of restricted sources. Events follow committed case state through a **Moderation-owned durable outbox** where relevant; Message Queue only transports. Consumers tolerate duplication, reordering and gaps, reconcile by source/version and never infer exactly-once distributed effects. Source owners emit their own authoritative resource-change events. Keep direction through host-assembled public ports, not direct cyclic package imports.

**Shared context, scoped per surface:** tenant and actor/service principal with authentication assurance, resource owner/type/opaque ID and expected revision, case and action IDs, effective policy/constraint version and jurisdiction, Clock-based event/effective/deadline time, evidence provenance/classification/expiry, correlation/idempotency, result status, and explicit `unknown` or `unsupported` outcomes. No universal shared DTO. Enforce stable contract versions and migration mappings; a changed policy must not retroactively rewrite the historical basis of a decided case.

## Error handling, diagnostics, Audit and Reporting — approved public contract boundary

**Owner-approved conceptual cooperation (22 November 2026).** These are public, host-composed interactions, not implemented integrations, imports of another owner's tables or a mandatory synchronous dependency. [Error Normalisation](../../libraries/error-normalisation/stateless-library-record.md) is a **pure, framework-independent stateless library**, not a service that collects or sends events.

| Cooperating library / capability | Moderation provides (public output) | Moderation receives (public input) | Authority and failure rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Error Normalisation** | Source-owned, versioned moderation error codes, qualified observed outcome, safe source metadata, approved mappings and disclosure profile, opaque case/operation correlation | Bounded safe standard code/category/message key, optional field and correlation references, sanitised public and diagnostic projections, advisory retry qualifier and explicit unknown/uncertain classification | Moderation alone owns whether intake, decision, appeal or enforcement committed and whether retry/reconciliation is safe. The library has no persistence, logging, Audit, reporting or automatic retries. Invalid/unrecognised errors get safe fallback; no raw evidence, source texts, secrets or protected existence leaked. |
| **Logging** | Allowlisted technical diagnostics: event code, category, severity, component/operation, safe correlation, latency, timeout, owner-adapter failures and retry/reconciliation problems; no raw reports/evidence | Diagnostic intake acknowledgement, rejection/throttling/unavailable status and authorised diagnostic query/delivery health | Logging owns sanitised technical records/sink delivery, **not** case history or proof of business success. Unavailable routine Logging must not prevent valid Moderation actions; critical safeguards remain independently enforced. |
| **Audit** | Risk-selected, attributable and minimised **committed** case creation, triage, decision, appeal, enforcement request/receipt/reversal, legal disclosure and sensitive evidence-access facts; separate significant denied/attempted actions with correct outcome classification; source event ID/version, tenant, trusted actor/assurance, policy and time | Accepted/pending/rejected/uncertain evidence-intake receipt, tamper-evidence/integrity verification, gap/coverage status, review reference and controlled evidential export | Moderation owns original case and case outbox; Audit owns received evidence and custody. Mandatory high-risk Audit evidence must use recoverable delivery or a fail-safe hold according to risk policy. Logging acceptance is never an Audit receipt; Audit acceptance does not prove source enforcement. |
| **Reporting** | Authorised, minimised, versioned case and event facts: report/decision/appeal types, case counts, source owner, policy scope, assigned deadlines and actual completion receipts, exposure/response time, false positives/reversals, backlog, provenance, coverage/freshness and missing/unknown statuses | Report definitions/catalogue, permitted filters, report execution status, derived aggregates, trend/KPI results, export references, and coverage/freshness limitations | Reporting owns projections, calculations and exports, not case adjudication or source restrictions. Metrics require denominators and time windows; unknown cases are not zero, source action request is not completed takedown. Reporting failure does not block Moderation's native lifecycle. |

**Flow:** A moderation command is authorised and validated; Moderation determines/commits its authoritative outcome and outbox fact. It may call the pure Error Normalisation library to return an appropriate safe error. Separately and purposefully it submits sanitised diagnostics to Logging, risk-selected accountable evidence to Audit and disclosure-scoped authoritative facts to Reporting. The host may route committed events via Message Queue, which does not change evidence or business ownership. All readers independently enforce tenant/actor permissions and data minimisation. Avoid circular runtime imports: independent ports/caller composition carry these interactions.

**Acceptance examples:** A stale enforcement receipt returns a safe conflict/uncertainty classification without pretending the action succeeded; a Logging outage does not erase the case decision; missing mandatory Audit custody remains pending or safely blocks a high-risk action as designed; Reporting shows partial/missing enforcement separately from completed effects; appeals/reversals change derived statistics without rewriting historical decisions; no cross-tenant correlation or sensitive case evidence leaks through any diagnostic, audit or report.

**Provider neutrality:** default native case management must not require a vendor AI moderation API. Providers are replaceable evidence producers, with declared identity, terms, versions, data-transfer location, charge/limits, outage, re-evaluation, migration, withdrawal and consent/retention controls. Import must preserve provenance and cannot falsely recast vendor verdicts as adjudications. Reconcile unknown asynchronous results; provider confidence is not a conviction. No global trusted peer reputation mechanism assumed.

## Security, compliance and adversarial challenge

- Apply OWASP ASVS 5 L2 with elevated applicable controls, segregated moderator duties, request-time Authorisation, high-assurance sensitive disclosure, audit of evidence viewing, protected reporter identity, careful notification wording, sanctions for abusive reporting, replay/race controls and tenant-scoped keys.
- Sensitive case records may implicate children, CSAM, NCII, harassment, domestic abuse or whistleblowing. Minimise payloads, use restricted evidence references rather than ordinary previews, avoid default downloading and raw telemetry, govern preservation, staff welfare, access reviews and deletion/hold exceptions. Do not promise that copying or viewing illegal material is inherently authorised.
- Qualified UK Online Safety Act obligations depend on **actual service type, jurisdiction, audience, risk category and operative duties**, supplied by Legal Compliance with qualified and dated source. Do not equate an installed module with compliance, content policy breach with an offence, a legal disclosure decision with a successful filing, or all private/E2EE communications with public searchable content.
- Private/E2EE reports require separately authorised user-submitted evidence and purpose-limited processing, not universal client-side scanning. False reports, compromise, brigading, spurious remote feeds and moderator conflict must support challenge and independent correction.
- **Approved MOD-D1:** Moderation prepares/validates/tracks a minimum statutory handoff; Legal Compliance qualifies duty, disclosure and preservation; a separately designated accountable filing process/person signs off and submits where legally applicable. Exact jurisdiction-specific filing actor and duty require legal qualification; MOD-O7 does not assert successful submission.
- Emergency holds are source-native and available if Moderation is unavailable. A stale case decision cannot override source-state revision, mandatory legal hold or newly revoked authorisation. Cases can close with explicitly unresolved downstream effects only under a documented escalation policy, never by concealing them.
- Persist minimal case/evidence references, action intents, receipts, deadline/disclosure and appeal trail, versioned policy basis and a durable outbox; record a future `STORAGE-REGISTER.md`. Use security classification, subject rights and lawful retention/erasure with qualified evidence preservation; no indefinite unrestricted case store.

## GUI, headless operation and host integration

**Approved presentation (22 November 2026):** Moderation requires an **optional operator and administration interface** when humans investigate cases, plus separately controlled user-facing report, receipt and appeal interfaces where participation calls for them. The capability remains useful as a headless service; do not require a production navigation item, a second UI framework or a UI-only capability. Theme Manager owns all semantic appearance; Moderation owns case presentation components and permission-sensitive case interaction; the application host owns routing, sidebar composition and tenant/context selection.

**Verified reference application:** [`nuxt-layers-test/application-test-template`](https://github.com/nuxt-layers-test/application-test-template) `README.md` and `nuxt.config.ts` on `master` (22 November 2026). It has a public home route `/` without an admin sidebar, an `/admin` test dashboard with expandable/collapsible responsive sidebar, Theme Manager at `/theme-manager`, a top-bar Appearance menu and a deliberately unauthenticated local Theme Manager test mode. Derived applications contribute their own navigation and scenarios; the shared shell is not to be modified. Theme Manager's [root `CONTRACT.md`](https://github.com/nuxt-layers/theme-manager/blob/master/CONTRACT.md) identifies present integration limitations; this is a representative interaction pattern, **not** proof that Moderation routes or a production IAM-compliant composition exist.

**Illustrative host navigation, not fixed API routes:** home `/` → test/admin area `/admin` → expanded **Capabilities** group → **Moderation** → capability dashboard at a host-selected path such as `/moderation`. In the derived moderation test application, the host registers the sidebar entry (accessible text and optional capability-supplied logo), route/menu placement, permission-aware visibility and trusted tenant context; navigating does not itself grant access. Production hosts may expose only the relevant report/appeal entry points and omit the moderator sidebar entirely. The logo returns home, Appearance remains host-wide and the reserved authentication area must be supplied by real IAM in production. Do not reuse Theme Manager's unauthenticated `test` mode outside isolated development acceptance.

**Visibility and action policy:** Navigation is merely discoverability. The host may omit unauthorised entries, while every screen, query and mutation independently obtains trustworthy caller/tenant context and Authorisation decisions through the selected IAM Integration/public port. Distinguish reporter, assigned investigator, decision-maker, appeal reviewer, supervisor, disclosure officer, auditor and platform operator. A source owner retains resource-native emergency hold controls. Higher-risk evidence view, legal disclosure, mass actions and reversal require suitable assurance, reason and separation of duties; an authorised appeal reviewer must not be the original adjudicator. A dashboard and case row must not disclose inaccessible cases, private resources or aggregate existence.

**Experience and operational states:** Safe no-data, no-permission, incompatible adapter, pending scanner, source revision conflict, partial/unknown enforcement, expired evidence, Audit intake gap, Reporting stale projection and provider outage states must be explicit, non-deceptive and actionable. Use Error Normalisation's safe message keys and source-owned outcome; sanitised technical diagnostics go to Logging and accountable sensitive viewing to Audit. Accessible status announcements and forms follow WCAG 2.2 AA/WAI-ARIA where applicable; en-GB default and i18n-ready catalogue, keyboard/focus, screen-reader, contrast through Theme Manager semantic tokens and mobile sidebar/tab behaviour are required. Avoid storing evidence, case descriptions or access credentials in browser persistent storage. Query only scoped summaries until an explicit authorised detail request.

**Configuration and acceptance:** The envisioned Nuxt capability publishes documented optional components/pages/message keys or a headless-only composition, with host-controlled navigation and independently disableable pages; do not promise a current package export. Future root `CONTRACT.md` must document public presentation contributions, permissions, headless behaviour and the ADR-0012 composition budget (one call and at most 50 host-specific lines), with no host-authored moderation stores. A derived `nuxt-layers-test/moderation-test` application (concept only, not created) can use the shared test template and synthetic fixtures to demonstrate navigation, report submission, queues, protected case review, independent appeals, source-owned enforcement reconciliation, legal handoff, unknown outcomes and accessible use. The representative test template's unauthenticated Theme Manager test mode is **never** an acceptance substitute for production server-side IAM.

| **Application security and interoperability** | [OWASP ASVS 5.0](https://asvs.owasp.org/) L2, elevated controls where risk requires; applicable IETF protocols including [RFC 9457](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9457.html) for HTTP problem details; [W3C ActivityPub](https://www.w3.org/TR/activitypub/) only if chosen by Federation | IAM/Authorisation own trusted caller/access decisions, owners enforce changes, Moderation uses versioned protected ports and case-owned outbox, explicit failed/unknown receipts and tenant isolation. | **Responsible automated assistance** | [NIST AI RMF 1.0](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) (voluntary); ISO/IEC 42001:2023 where AI governance applies | Content Security/providers own detection evidence; Moderation distinguishes confidence, limitations and version from final reasoned adjudication. **Additional voluntary risk/quality context:** ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 31000:2018 may guide process improvement and severity scoring; NIST CSF 2.0 may guide operational controls. Specialist child-protection reporting and terrorist-content arrangements require case-specific legally qualified determination and authorised provider contracts, not blanket mandatory scanning or automatic external disclosure. **Do not use unavailable or licensed ISO full text as vendored source.**

**Traceability rule:** Each applicable requirement records its issuing authority, edition/effective version, mandatory-versus-voluntary status, actor/tenant/service scope, owning capability, affected MOD inbound/outbound surfaces, permitted failure mode, and conceptual/implementation acceptance evidence. Keep the standards matrix as design guidance; Legal Compliance owns the binding legal applicability, while Audit/Logging/Reporting do not inherit case ownership.

## Source-complete, commercially permissive library selection

**Library selection rule (approved licensing preference):** Evaluate only currently maintained packages with publicly inspectable **current release source**, a genuine OSI-style permissive licence (prefer MIT/BSD/Apache-2.0), redistributable commercial use, no compulsory commercial runtime/key/service, and no lagging open-core edition. Check the **exact selected release tag, package licence, transitive dependencies and bundled binaries** before implementation; a repository's top-level licence alone does not certify the full dependency tree. These are researched **candidates, not dependencies selected for installation**.

| Library (repository) | Licence | Useful role | Adoption boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Zod](https://github.com/colinhacks/zod) | MIT | Runtime validation of reporter submissions, policy versions, owner-action receipts and exposed public shapes | **Preferred** validation choice when the ecosystem already uses Zod; schema validation does not make external claims trusted |
| [node-postgres / pg](https://github.com/brianc/node-postgres) | MIT | PostgreSQL transactional case stores, revision checks, idempotency, outbox and receipt reconciliation | **Preferred** through capability-owned persistence adapter and host-supplied pool; no shared persistence owner |
| [Kysely](https://github.com/kysely-org/kysely) | MIT | Optional typed SQL composition and safer query maintenance | Adopt only if raw `pg` becomes unwieldy; keep schema/migrations and portability within Moderation |
| [XState](https://github.com/statelyai/xstate) | MIT | Explicit, inspectable state transitions for case and appeal tracks | **Optional** for genuine lifecycle complexity; stable v5 rather than November 2026 v6 beta; do not replace durable transaction logic or Workflow |
| [Ajv](https://github.com/ajv-validator/ajv) | MIT | JSON Schema validation at external peer/provider boundaries | Optional only where shared JSON Schema interoperability requires it; avoid duplicating Zod for all inputs |
| [TanStack Table, Vue adapter](https://github.com/TanStack/table) | MIT | Optional accessible-capable headless sorting, filtering and paging of moderation queues | UI mechanics only: server controls access, tenant filtering and pagination; no source ownership |
| [VueUse](https://github.com/vueuse/vueuse) | MIT | Optional reactive UI utilities for moderator screens | Use selectively; prevent sensitive evidence/cases entering browser persistent storage |
| [Vitest](https://github.com/vitest-dev/vitest) and [fast-check](https://github.com/dubzzz/fast-check) | MIT | Behaviour, property and invariant testing, including state transitions and retry/idempotency | Development-only; CI/user runs tests. Prefer established platform test baseline |
| [Playwright](https://github.com/microsoft/playwright) | Apache-2.0 | Accessible reporter/moderator workflows, authorisation and appeal E2E acceptance | Development-only, locally installed browsers and CI; browser redistribution licences checked separately |

**Cooperating capability integrations rather than Moderation dependencies:** [Pino](https://github.com/pinojs/pino) (MIT) belongs to Logging, [OpenTelemetry JS](https://github.com/open-telemetry/opentelemetry-js) (Apache-2.0) to Observability. Scheduler/Temporal, Message Queue/pgmq, Workflow, Clock, rich-text, Content Security, Export, Audit and IAM retain their own implementation and provider choices; Moderation consumes only published contracts. [p-queue](https://github.com/sindresorhus/p-queue) is MIT but provides in-process concurrency only, **not durable retry or scheduling**; avoid adding it unless a bounded local-only operation actually requires it. Likewise do not introduce a second queue, search engine, generic policy engine, separate graph DB or hosted AI moderation dependency without an independently demonstrated need.

**Deliberately excluded:** Redis/Elasticsearch/Neo4j-style commercial/open-core dependencies under the stated source-completeness policy; source-incomplete, cloud-only, source-available-with-restrictions and proprietary classifier products; unmaintained OSS; immature betas as production defaults. No vendor reputation feed may produce an automatic legally authoritative finding.

**Minimal recommended initial stack:** PostgreSQL + existing host pool + Zod; optional Kysely for query complexity, optional stable XState for case statecharts, optional TanStack Table for operator UI; existing Nuxt/Theme Manager, IAM Integration, Clock and other capability contracts for cross-cutting concerns. **Do not install all candidates pre-emptively.** Verify Node/TS compatibility, exact lockfile revisions, licence notices, vulnerabilities, maintained release activity and consumer acceptance before implementation.
