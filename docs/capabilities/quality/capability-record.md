**Classification:** Domain capability; independently useful without Manufacturing. **Conceptual status:** **Owner-approved, complete for conceptual design** (21–22 November 2026): QTY-01–12, four authority/sector/certification/sharing clarifications, standards framework, permissive-library selection and reciprocal Logging/Audit/Reporting interfaces. ## Purpose and authority — QTY-01 / QTY-02 (approved)

**Quality** independently plans, records, reviews and traces evidence about whether a supplied product, batch, process, service, repair or other defined subject meets **versioned acceptance criteria**. It manages qualified inspections/tests, deviations and non-conformances, authorised dispositions, corrective/preventive actions (CAPA) and quality-related approvals. It works with external laboratories, customers and suppliers without assuming they hold platform accounts; each party and attestation is scope-qualified and checked.

**Native owned records:** versioned quality plan and sampling/inspection requirements for a defined subject; inspection/test job and checklist; measured observations and source evidence; finding/non-conformance, severity and affected scope; quality disposition and approvals; retest/reinspection; CAPA with effectiveness assessment; quality case trace/history and correction chains. Own **quality acceptance evidence/decision for the specific quality gate**. Avoid a second generic document store: keep evidence metadata, signed/hash-qualified attachments and references to their document/media owners.

**Approved Manufacturing/Quality boundary:** Manufacturing **retains operational production-quality gates, work-order progress, routing, hold-in-execution and rework instruction/approval**, and is accountable for whether production can proceed. Where a **separate Quality gate is configured or required**, Quality owns the independent inspection result and technical quality disposition, which Manufacturing must consume before executing its own decision. Neither party can unilaterally erase the other's refusal; the stricter applicable gate controls. For **simple sites without Quality selected**, Manufacturing may retain its existing native production inspection and decision mechanism; this does not create an implicit Quality dependency or second master. Once Quality is designated owner for a given gate and effective revision, Manufacturing must not secretly issue a second contradictory technical acceptance. A changeover needs deliberate authority mapping and historical reconciliation. This conditional division preserves native Manufacturing inspections when Quality is absent; transition per designated gate requires an authorised effective revision and historical reconciliation.

**Other immutable ownership:** Product controls designs, product identity, specifications, units and engineering revisions. Inventory alone commits physical stock holds/quarantine/release, quantity-bearing transfers, lot/serial stock genealogy and stock availability. Manufacturing owns transformation/work execution, production-specific causal trace and production rework instructions. Warehouse owns physical handling work; Supplier/Procurement owns commercial supplier and purchase decisions; Asset owns equipment identity, maintenance and calibration lifecycle; Facility owns site/zone safety restrictions; qualified external bodies retain any legally reserved sign-off or accreditation authority. Quality's requested hold is not an Inventory hold; an accepted inspection is not proof stock was released or work completed. Quality neither sets law nor issues universally valid legal or accreditation certificates.

## Native operation and lifecycle — QTY-03 / QTY-04 (approved)

Operate headlessly without Manufacturing, external laboratories or specific equipment. Quality can inspect incoming goods, external services, repairs or supplier delivery using an authorised subject reference and acceptance criteria. **Plan:** draft → reviewed → approved/effective → superseded/retired, retaining pinned revisions for existing inspections. **Inspection:** requested → assigned → in-progress → evidence pending → completed with pass/fail/indeterminate results → independently reviewed/accepted or disputed → closed/corrected through explicit linked addendum; no silent rewriting of signed results. **Non-conformance:** opened → contained/impact assessed → disposition proposed → approved/refused → action under way → verified/closed or reopened, with correlated owner-side restrictions. **CAPA:** initiated → root-cause assessed → action approved → implemented → effectiveness checked → closed/ineffective/reopened. Statuses are illustrative; qualified approvals and legal constraints may demand stricter steps.

A failed or uncertain mandatory quality gate must remain **blocked/unknown**, not implicitly passed. Scope holds may be recommended to the owning domain immediately; only that domain's confirmed change is presented as an actual restriction. Risk-based sampling is permitted only under an authorised plan; absent mandatory samples/results cannot be treated as success. Configurable tolerance cannot weaken a statutory or contractual floor. Overrides need designated authority, separation of duties when necessary, reason/evidence and non-overridable restrictions.

## Idealised public inbound contract — QTY-05; Q1–Q5 (approved)

Quality receives **commands**, **source-owned facts**, **qualified evidence** and **effective policy** through authorised, versioned public interfaces. The relevant owner supplies each fact; Quality checks whether it is suitable for the specified inspection. A received event never grants permission.

| Source and necessity | Inputs | Validation and absence |
|---|---|---|
| **Caller/host (required for change)** | Trusted tenant/subject, assurance, permission decision, action, target, expected revision, reason, correlation and idempotency | Deny unauthorised or cross-tenant requests; stale revision is conflict, not overwrite; reused key with changed payload is conflict |
| **Subject owner, including Product (required when criteria refer to it)** | Subject identity, approved specification, tolerances, units, version and effective scope | Missing, withdrawn, ambiguous or contradictory facts block final acceptance; pin historic version |
| **Inspector or competent external laboratory (conditional evidence)** | Measurements, samples, method, test results, report reference, signature, traceability, uncertainty and provenance | Check authenticity, evidence coverage and method; a provider receipt is not a Quality pass |
| **Manufacturing (conditional)** | Work order, operation, gate, revision and execution evidence | Missing required production facts leave that gate indeterminate; no inference of work completion |
| **Inventory, Warehouse, Logistics (conditional)** | Lot/serial, receipt, physical position, custody/shipping and owner-confirmed restrictions | Pending hold is not committed quarantine or movement |
| **Asset and Facility (conditional)** | Device identity, calibration/maintenance and site safety/suitability facts | Missing valid calibration or site evidence blocks only affected qualified action |
| **Qualification or competent issuer (conditional)** | Inspector award/credential, competence scope, issuer authority, effective validity and revocation | Quality decides method-specific suitability and independence; credential does not prove accreditation; local qualified evidence remains possible without Qualification |
| **Supplier/Procurement, Order/customer (conditional)** | Delivery, supplier/customer, contractual criteria and service context | Validate consent/authority/scope; no implied commercial or identity change |
| **Configuration, Legal Compliance, Privacy, Clock (required where applicable)** | Approved versioned policy, law/contract floor, delegation, retention/disclosure, time and jurisdiction | Missing mandatory rule fails closed for affected act; personal preference cannot waive independent review or law |
| **Integrations/external provider (optional)** | Qualified request/receipt, external provider outcome, reconciliation reference and status | Unavailable/uncertain stays pending or unknown; native operation must remain possible |

**Commands:** approve/revise/retire plans and criteria; request/assign/record/review/correct inspections; attest observations; open/assess/dispose non-conformances; request retest or cross-owner restriction; start/approve/verify/reopen CAPA; reconcile external and domain-owner acknowledgements. Validate permission, assurance, independent reviewer where applicable, expected revision and idempotency. Return *refused*, *conflict*, *accepted for processing*, *committed* or *pending/unknown*, with a safe code and retriable reference when appropriate. No duplicate approvals, tests, holds or CAPA from replay.

**Qualified evidence:** Pin subject, plan, method, sampler, instrument/qualification, rule edition, actor/issuer, uncertainty, observed/effective/recorded times, source revision and correction lineage. Incomplete mandatory evidence never becomes a pass. A prior signed decision is corrected by a linked attributable amendment, not silent rewriting.

## Idealised public outbound contract — QTY-06; Q1–Q5 (approved)

Quality publishes controlled **queries**, **command outcomes**, **reports/evidence** and **committed events**. Its technical findings remain its authority; a recipient decides its own operational actions.

| Public output; consumers | Meaning and limits |
|---|---|
| **Plans and gate requirements** — inspectors, Manufacturing, domain owners | Approved criteria, samples/methods, effective scope, revision, competence and required evidence; no modification of Product specifications |
| **Inspection/test results and dispositions** — Manufacturing, Inventory, Warehouse, Product, authorised customers/suppliers | Qualified *pass/fail/indeterminate* for the precisely inspected subject and revision, evidence, approver, severity, valid-time, status and provenance; **not** production completion, stock release or externally issued certification |
| **Non-conformance and CAPA** — subject owners, Workflow, authorised case users | Controlled defect scope, disposition and corrective/effectiveness state; other owners retain product design, production work and commercial changes |
| **Restriction requests and confirmations** — Inventory, Manufacturing, Warehouse, other action owners | Idempotent hold/release/rework **request** and its separately owner-confirmed *committed/refused/pending/unknown* acknowledgement and revision; never label a request as an actual restriction |
| **Evidence and authorised disclosures** — inspectors, auditor, customer, supplier, external body | Purpose/recipient-specific reports, attestations, revisions, source references and permitted redactions; no blanket accreditation or unauthorised raw records |
| **Measures** — Reporting, authorised dashboard | Inspected/failed/indeterminate counts, clear denominators, sample coverage, first-pass yield, NCR classes and CAPA effectiveness/age; include source revisions, missing coverage, event/effective times and corrections |
| **Accountable assertions and operational events** — Audit, Message Queue, Workflow, Notifications | Post-commit, source-owned plan/result/NCR/CAPA/restriction-request/correction changes; event ID, tenant, subject/revision, qualified actor/outcome, occurred and committed time, correlation and allowed evidence references |
| **Safe technical diagnostics** — Logging/Observability | Minimal codes, classification, severity and opaque correlation; never raw client designs, credentials or full laboratory files |

**Queries:** Authorised plans, inspections, evidence, dispositions, NCR/CAPA, pending restriction acknowledgements and revision history return source, freshness, completeness, valid-time and disclosure limitations. No cross-tenant existence/count leakage. **Events:** originate in Quality's durable outbox after its own state commit; Message Queue transports them, and consumers deduplicate, reorder/reconcile and handle revisions. A transport receipt is not an Audit receipt or a domain-owner execution confirmation; no distributed exactly-once guarantee. **Exports and reporting** are source-qualified projections, not another technical verdict. Exact types, exported functions and codes are deferred to an implemented root `CONTRACT.md`.

## Cross-capability contracts and separation — QTY-07; Q1–Q5 (approved)

| Partner | It supplies to Quality | Quality supplies; ownership and absence |
|---|---|---|
| **Product / subject owner** | Approved designs, characteristics, tolerances, versions | Findings and design-improvement requests; Product alone changes product masters; conditional on subject |
| **Manufacturing** | Work/operation/gate and execution version | Independent technical verdict where designated; Manufacturing retains work, native checks when Quality absent, rework and operational gates |
| **Inventory** | Lot, stock state, actual hold/release acknowledgement and revision | Qualified restriction request and evidence; Inventory alone commits stock movements, quarantine and availability |
| **Warehouse / Logistics** | Receipt, handling, shipment, custody | Findings and operational restriction requests; never a duplicate handling ledger |
| **Supplier/Procurement, Order/customer** | Party/transaction references and acceptance requirements | Qualified findings, supplier improvements and scoped customer evidence; no commercial transaction or automatic platform identity |
| **Asset / Facility** | Equipment condition/calibration and site restrictions | Measurement suitability and findings; Asset retains device lifecycle, Facility retains site safety authority |
| **Qualification / issuer** | Award/credential validity, scope, provenance and revocation | Method-specific competence requirements and accepted-evidence assessment; no credential changes or implied accredited issuer status |
| **IAM, Configuration, Clock, Legal Compliance, Privacy** | Trusted caller/access/assurance, scoped policies, effective time and legal/disclosure duties | Protected operations and accountable control evidence; these owners retain their respective decisions |
| **Workflow, Scheduler, Notifications, Integrations** | Approval/task coordination, execution/delivery status, external provider evidence | Approved tasks, due dates, messages and external testing requests; optional, no transfer of technical authority |
| **Error Normalisation, Logging, Audit, Reporting, Observability, Message Queue** | Safe error projections, receipts/health, evidence custody, analytic outputs and transport acknowledgements | See the distinct public interfaces below; none owns Quality state |

Relationships are **host-composed through public interfaces**, not mandatory package imports or private database access. They must be directional and jointly acyclic. Quality works independently without Manufacturing, Reporting, external laboratory or GUI. Missing a required fact blocks only the qualified operation that needs it; none of the partners can convert uncertainty into technical acceptance.

## Error Normalisation, Logging, Audit and Reporting — approved public contract

Quality remains the authority for inspection results, dispositions and CAPA. These partners have separate purposes; a log line, audit receipt or chart cannot prove a product's conformity.

| Partner | Quality supplies (partner input) | Partner returns (Quality input); limits |
|---|---|---|
| **`error-normalisation` stateless library** | Source-owned error/outcome, code namespace, approved mapping/version, disclosure purpose, safe context | Pure versioned safe code/category/message key, field hint, advisory retry and separate public/diagnostic projections; it creates no events, permission, log, Audit evidence, state or recovery decision |
| **Logging** | Bounded sanitised validation/import/provider/timeout faults, severity, source code and opaque correlation | Accepted/rejected/throttled/unavailable/uncertain diagnostic intake, permitted diagnostic reference and sink health/loss. Logging owns processing and routing, not the business outcome |
| **Audit** | Risk-selected signed/denied/changed inspection, NCR disposition, override, CAPA closure and correction assertion; committed source ID/revision, trusted actor, tenant, subject, event time, outcome and allowed evidence references | Accepted/pending/rejected/uncertain *evidence* receipt; integrity, custody, completeness/reconciliation and review status. Audit owns evidence custody, not the Quality verdict |
| **Reporting** | Authorised revisioned inspection counts, denominators, sampling/defect and CAPA measures, time, coverage, missing/indeterminate facts and correction events | Report catalogue/definitions, run status, permitted trends/aggregates, snapshot cutoff, freshness/completeness and export/delivery result. Reporting owns derived reports, not inspection data |

**Errors versus business outcomes:** A failed inspection is an authoritative Quality *fail*, not automatically a software exception. Invalid measurement syntax may refuse a command and produce a sanitised Logging diagnostic. A laboratory timeout leaves the affected result pending/indeterminate; its safe normalised error cannot establish remote non-commitment or authorise an unsafe retry. A denied critical approval yields a safe public response and separately risk-selected Audit evidence. Corrected quality findings generate revision-linked committed events for Audit reconciliation and Reporting reaggregation. Reporting must never use log-line counts alone as authoritative defect totals or double-count replays.

**Failure rules:** Normalisation is locally callable without other capabilities. Ordinary Logging or Reporting outages must not undo a valid Quality transaction. Mandatory Audit evidence must be durably recoverable or fail closed/escalate according to approved risk policy; a missing receipt cannot be fabricated. Separate source commit, event transport, Audit intake and evidence verification. Sanitise secrets, personal data, proprietary designs and raw NDT/laboratory files; authorised consumers independently enforce tenancy, purpose, retention and disclosure. No direct library-to-service call, hidden coupling or private-store sharing.

## Shared context, safety and privacy — QTY-08 / QTY-09 (approved)

Every result must pin applicable criteria/method/subject revisions, tenant and permitted recipient scope, identity/qualification of approver or trusted producer, instrument/method and calibration reference where material, units, timestamps (occurred/recorded/effective), status, source provenance, contradiction/uncertainty, evidence hashes/references and correction lineage. Rights to view a result, a finding, a trade-secret specification or a customer-branded certificate may differ. Enforce server-side Authorisation, justified assurance/step-up, segregation of duties and tenant/customer/client isolation. Supplier or client being an external party **does not itself make it a platform user**; onboarding should resolve authorised existing party/identity references rather than create duplicates. Redact sensitive methods, client designs, health-related or other protected evidence as required; retention and disclosure follow legally qualified policy, not blanket permanence. Quality measures must not become unauthorised worker-performance surveillance.

**ISO 9001 governance belongs to Quality, not Manufacturing.** Quality is the conceptual owner of reusable quality-management controls, inspection/conformity policies, non-conformance handling, corrective/improvement evidence and quality-specific review/approval records, using the applicable [ISO 9001 quality-management standard](../../../RESOURCES.md#iso-9001) as a design reference. Quality publishes qualified requirements, gates, test findings and approvals; Manufacturing owns the resulting production action, and Inventory commits physical stock restrictions. Quality must not take over an organisation's overall management-system accountability or imply that the software or a factory is ISO 9001 certified. Keep standard editions, effective scope, applicability and evidential mappings explicit.

Apply risk-based inspection, immutable approval history via append-only corrections where appropriate, versioned standards and defensible sampling; support extra competence, independence and accreditation checks where mandated (e.g. NDT, safety-critical testing). Claim no blanket ISO 9001, ISO/IEC 17025, industry-specific or regulatory certification from the software design.

## Standards and conformity reference framework — owner-approved (21 November 2026)

**Design rule:** `quality` provides a **standards-aware, versioned requirement and evidence model**, not a universal certification or a built-in reproduction of copyrighted standard texts. Resolve standard **identifier, publisher, edition, amendment, applicable clause/reference, jurisdiction/contractual scope, effective dates, source provenance, controlling approval and transition state**. The host/domain policy supplies which profiles apply; qualified Legal Compliance supplies jurisdictional statutory interpretation; Product/Manufacturing/Asset retain their own source records. Avoid hard-coding particular ISO editions into API types, domain lifecycles or mandatory provider adapters. An organisation may have a valid transitional certificate under an earlier edition; preserve evidence against that edition without silently claiming conformity with a newer one.

**Standards selection and update discipline (approved design guidance, 22 November 2026):** Keep a small default standards catalogue, with optional profiles selected by product/sector, jurisdiction, contract, method and risk. Record authority (publisher, national adoption, competent certifier), edition, amendment, valid-time, applicability, approval, evidence method, expected competence/independence, traceability and known transition status. A publisher's new edition must not automatically invalidate a historical inspection or rewrite a legal duty: apply a controlled transition and revisit affected prospective plans. Consult Legal Compliance for statutory meaning and appropriate source owners for design/calibration/credentials; reference standards rather than copying paid or copyrighted texts. ISO 9001:2026 and ISO 19011:2026 were publisher-confirmed as published as of 22 November 2026 (https://www.iso.org/standard/9001; https://www.iso.org/standard/19011). UKAS policies and edition transitions are independently verified where accredited services are claimed (https://www.ukas.com/accreditation/accreditation-standards/).

**Public contract consequences:** support controlled qualification profiles, effective-dated rules and revisions, linked acceptance criteria, units and sampling plans, sources and competence, technical sign-off, independent approval/override, statuses including **indeterminate**, immutable correction lineage, context-appropriate evidence disclosures and source-owner hold requests with explicit acknowledgement. Audit receives accountable quality decisions, Reporting consumes scoped quality measures, Legal Compliance owns legal interpretation, and Quality retains the technical quality verdict. Missing accredited scope or qualification blocks only the affected qualified act.

**Status:** **Owner-approved standards framework on 21 November 2026, expanded by owner-authorised best-practice guidance on 22 November 2026**, alongside the separately approved QTY-01–12. External standards can be copyrighted or paywalled; store reference metadata/authorised extracts, not copied full texts. Version and regulatory applicability must be revalidated during implementation and when publisher revisions occur.

## Open-source implementation candidates — owner-approved selection and upstream verification (22 November 2026)

**Strict eligibility and ownership:** Only consider libraries whose **complete applicable upstream implementation** is publicly inspectable and available to everyone under commercial-use-permissive **MIT, ISC, BSD or Apache-2.0** terms, without source-available restrictions, community/enterprise feature gates, paid activation, delayed source releases or dependence on a proprietary flagship. A published repository and permissive top-level licence are necessary **but not sufficient**: before an implementation pin, verify the selected tag against its release artefact, all bundled/transitive components, notices, provenance, vulnerabilities, maintenance and licensing. A hosted paid convenience service does not itself disqualify a library if the complete library and required functionality are available under the permissive licence. No package is installed or committed to as an implementation dependency by this conceptual approval. Quality owns business decisions, records, public contracts and durable events; libraries are replaceable support components.

**Preferred, source-available permissive shortlist (upstream checked 22 November 2026):**

| Library / upstream source | Permissive licence | Use in Quality | Selection guidance |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Ajv](../../../RESOURCES.md#ajv) | MIT | JSON Schema validation of versioned public evidence, plans, measurements and commands | **Preferred** when JSON Schema is the shared published contract. Maintain schema compatibility and use patched releases; do not permit untrusted custom code/regex without bounds |
| [Zod](../../../RESOURCES.md#zod) | MIT | TypeScript-friendly command/form validation | **Preferred alternative** for TypeScript-native surfaces; avoid competing Ajv/Zod sources of truth without explicit generation and conformance |
| [decimal.js](../../../RESOURCES.md#decimaljs) | MIT | Arbitrary-precision measurements, acceptance limits, decimal rounding and reproducible comparisons | **Preferred** for measurement precision; units, conversions, tolerances and uncertainty policy remain Quality's validated method, not implicit library defaults |
| [simple-statistics](../../../RESOURCES.md#simple-statistics) | ISC | Descriptive statistics, sampling summaries, process-control calculations where explicitly verified | **Preferred for basic statistics**, subject to numerical method, confidence/sample-size, reproducibility and regression evidence |
| [Apache ECharts](../../../RESOURCES.md#apache-echarts) | Apache-2.0 | Inspection/QC dashboards and defect trends through the presentation/Reporting boundary | **Preferred where richer plots are needed**; ASF publishes full source releases. Calculations remain server/domain-owned and charts require accessible data tables |
| [Chart.js](../../../RESOURCES.md#chartjs) | MIT | Simpler inspection trends and charts through shared UI/Reporting | **Alternative to ECharts**; do not install both by default |
| [OpenTelemetry JavaScript](../../../RESOURCES.md#opentelemetry-js) | Apache-2.0 | Tracing, operational metrics and diagnostic correlation | **Reuse platform Observability**, not a separate quality analytics authority; sanitise all exported fields |

**Conditionally useful and not mandatory:** [json-rules-engine](../../../RESOURCES.md#json-rules-engine) (ISC) is a transparent declarative condition evaluator, **not** an approver or a safety/legal policy engine. Only accept bounded, versioned, schema-validated rule profiles; prevent arbitrary tenant callbacks and ambiguous asynchronous results; fail closed when a qualified gate is unresolved. [PDFKit](../../../RESOURCES.md#pdfkit) (MIT) has a publicly available library and recent releases, but Quality should consume the shared **Export** contract instead of creating a competing file-rendering authority; include accessibility, signatures and provenance checks in the owner contract. [jStat](../../../RESOURCES.md#jstat) (MIT) is a possible advanced-statistics alternative only after release-cadence, maintenance and algorithm-specific evidence is established; do not treat it as a verified preferred active dependency.

**Not recommended under the strict *active and stable* eligibility gate:** [`@zxing/browser`](../../../RESOURCES.md#zxingbrowser) and its [`@zxing/library`](../../../RESOURCES.md#zxinglibrary) decoding dependency may be permissively licensed, and the browser wrapper has recent releases, but the underlying library explicitly says **maintenance mode only** and requests new maintainers. Do not count a fresh wrapper release as proof of active core development. Treat scanning as an optional host/shared UI integration, with accessible manual entry and provider substitution; do not pin this family until a maintained, fully permissive implementation is demonstrated. Exclude Neo4j, Redis and Elasticsearch under the user's full-upstream, no-open-core preference; no additional graph/search/cache product is needed. Avoid making a blanket permissive-only recommendation for `mathjs` without auditing its actual distributed third-party licensing; an inactive PDF renderer is likewise not preferred.

**Publisher evidence and currency:** The upstream repositories and release catalogues show Ajv, Zod, simple-statistics, ECharts, Chart.js, PDFKit and OpenTelemetry have contemporary release/maintenance signals as of this review. Full upstream source and stated licences are observable, but this does **not** establish a complete independent audit of all transitive dependencies, software bills of materials or distributable artefact equivalence. ECharts' Apache source-release catalogue is particularly useful evidence of full publication. Relevant upstream records: https://github.com/ajv-validator/ajv/releases ; https://github.com/colinhacks/zod/releases ; https://github.com/simple-statistics/simple-statistics/releases ; https://releases.apache.org/catalog/echarts ; https://github.com/chartjs/Chart.js/releases ; https://github.com/open-telemetry/opentelemetry-js/releases ; https://github.com/foliojs/pdfkit/releases ; https://github.com/zxing-js/library/blob/master/README.md .

**Integration consequences:** Use existing platform validation, Reporting, Export and Observability contracts where available. Give each statistical output explicit formula/method version, input revision, units, sample definition, precision, uncertainty, timestamps and provenance; independently verify specialist SPC, NDT, regulatory and accredited calculations rather than assuming generic statistics utilities certify them. A rules-engine result, scan, chart or PDF **never** constitutes signed technical approval or another domain's physical hold. Keep approved public Quality interfaces independent of vendor-specific SDK types.

## Advisory GUI and host composition — QTY-10; Q1–Q5 (approved)

**Optional presentation; mandatory headless equivalence.** Quality may supply separable screens built exclusively on its public contract. The application owns navigation, routes, access context and common layout; Quality owns records, validation and server-side decisions.
