## Purpose, ownership and exclusions

Subscription manages **recurring commercial agreements and the entitlement decisions derived from them**, independently of payment processing or invoice production. It supports tenant, group, person or organisation-scoped service access and externally billable offerings without conflating contracting party, bill-to party, service beneficiary, Identity membership, deployed service and user permission. It is a durable domain owner, not a stateless transformation library or a replacement for application onboarding.

**Authoritative records (proposed):** versioned plan definitions and benefit/limit schedules; offer acceptance and subscription agreement references; subscriber and beneficiary scope, legal counterparty reference and validity; recurring term and renewal schedule; trial, scheduled start, active, grace, paused, suspended, cancellation-pending, terminated and expired states; entitlement grants/limits and evaluated availability; seat allocations *within contractual licensed limits* (not Identity membership); provider-facing source references, corrections, migration history and an atomic capability-owned outbox. Terms, effective-time policy, revision and provenance accompany every consequential transition.

**Does not own:** Pricing tariffs/quote arithmetic; Billing's rated charges, billing-run status or proration; Invoice's issued documents; Payment Gateway's payment operations, settlement, disputes or card credentials; Accounting's ledger; legal entity or payer verification; Membership's scheme admission/qualification; Identity's tenants/groups/memberships; Authentication sessions; Authorisation access decisions; service provisioning, quota metering, usage sources or an external SaaS provider's authoritative account state. Contract documents may be published through Publisher without transferring executable term authority. Mere payment, accepted order, configured entitlement or successful provisioning is not independently evidence of another's success.

**Commercial boundary (approved SUB-09–SUB-10):** A plan defines an offering; an agreement pins accepted terms; an entitlement indicates scoped commercial eligibility, **not** an IAM grant or proof of provisioning. Preserve locally accepted terms or verified, externally authoritative contracts without inventing another legal agreement. Membership admission is independent of subscription/payment. Host composition coordinates provisioning, not commercial decisions.

## Native service and lifecycle

1. Publish approved plan versions with explicit scope, availability, currency/tariff references, eligibility, capacity units, limits, trial/renewal/grace/cancellation policy, effective windows and immutable historical versions. Subscriptions pin the accepted terms; later plan changes require explicit permitted migration and notice/consent where applicable.
2. Verify authorised acceptance and beneficiary/counterparty resolution, versioned offer, price/terms evidence, applicable legal and privacy constraints, effective dates and idempotency. Create **pending** agreement until all mandatory activation conditions are independently verified. An agreement may be free; payment is conditional, not universally required. Where an external system is the contract authority, record the verified foreign contract and ownership designation without fabricating local assent.
3. Activate only under source-approved, effective policy with verified prerequisites; independently evaluate scoped entitlements at a specified as-of time. Pending, refused, reversed and **unknown** payments are never treated as settled. A trial, agreed invoice-credit arrangement or explicitly approved grace policy may independently permit time-limited access, with expiry and enforcement evidenced. Offline bank-transfer confirmation comes from an independently verified receipt/Accounting evidence source, not the presence of an Invoice alone. Trial, grace, paused and suspended entitlements are **explicit policy outcomes**, not implicit active access. (Owner-approved SUB-11, SUB-13.)
4. Schedule renewal decisions with Clock/Scheduler, verify eligibility and agreed renewal terms, request charge assessment from Billing when relevant, and apply explicit expiry, grace, cancellation and non-renewal rules. An attempted renewal, assessed charge or pending payment never silently extends the contractual term. Late or reversed payment leads to policy-governed reassessment and a durable correction, not silent rewriting.
5. Support authorised upgrades/downgrades, seat/limit changes, migration, pause/resume, cancellation (immediate or end-of-term), termination, reversal/correction and source-reference reconciliation with effective dates. Subscription owns licensed allowances and versioned seat allocations/reservations; Identity still owns users/memberships and service owners meter actual usage. Enforce capacity atomically with conflict-safe reservation/release, never promise available quota based on stale telemetry. Define overage as reject, notify, or separately approved billable excess, never an implied purchase. Financial adjustments are requested of their owners; do not manufacture a refund. (Owner-approved SUB-12.)
6. Preserve reproducible state history and independent audit facts, stable idempotency keys, monotonic domain revisions and an owned transactional outbox. Event delivery can duplicate, delay or reorder; consumers read the current authorised version. Distinguish native and externally managed agreements with an explicit per-agreement authority designation. An external provider may execute its own lifecycle but its webhook must be verified and reconciled before local entitlement changes; never let two systems independently commit the same lifecycle transition. Migration uses dry-run comparison, verified identity/terms/payment mandate mapping, agreed cutover, replay-safe idempotency, one renewal owner per billing period and reversible/quarantined exceptions rather than simultaneous renewal authority. (Owner-approved SUB-15, SUB-16.)

**Lifecycle:** `draft/pending → trial | scheduled → active → grace | paused | suspended | cancellation-pending → renewed | expired | terminated`, with strictly authorised transitions and correction history. Invalid/unavailable prerequisites retain a precise pending, rejected or unknown outcome; unknown external effects cannot be converted to active on timeout. Absence of optional Billing/Payment permits only explicitly supported free/offline arrangements; paid activation requiring their verified facts fails closed. Missing authoritative persistent store, source access decision or essential terms blocks mutation. Background scheduling outages require reconciliation, not backdated assertions without evidence.

## Idealised public input contract

These **logical** names define service responsibility, not verified exported functions or HTTP routes. Every protected request carries trusted caller, tenant/beneficiary scope, resource authorisation, expected revision, stable idempotency identifier, requested effective time and source-policy/version provenance as appropriate. All external references and provider callbacks are untrusted until verified; exact decimal money/currency belongs to the commercial owner.

| Input / command or port | Supplier and required information | Validation and absence semantics |
|---|---|---|
| `publishPlanVersion`, `retirePlan` | Authorised commercial owner; offering, entitlements/units, eligibility, schedule, legal terms, start/end policy and price references | Enforce scoped approvals, immutable published history and effective-date conflicts; no unauthorised retroactivity |
| `proposeSubscription`, `acceptTerms` | Host/Order/customer; plan revision, subscriber, verified agreement authority, beneficiary, terms consent, selected offer, scope and idempotency | No membership/contracting authority inferred from tenant tree or payment; ambiguous terms remain pending |
| `activate`, `renew`, `changePlan`, `changeAllocation` | Authorised actor or scheduled host; expected revision, effective-date policy, eligibility, acceptance and independent funding evidence where required | Reject stale facts, licence overflow and conflicting allocations; evaluated state only after committed change |
| `pause`, `resume`, `cancel`, `terminate`, `correct` | Authorised subscriber/operator, reason, permitted effective date, assurance/approval, consumer protections | Respect notice, legal obligations, committed-period constraints and irreversible history |
| `reconcileExternal`, `applySourceEvidence` | Provider adapters/Integrations, Billing/Payment Gateway/Invoice where relevant; signed or otherwise verified origin, current revision, source identity, effective time and certainty | Ignore duplicates, quarantine contradictions and out-of-order effects; payment evidence alone does not confer entitlement |
| `evaluateEntitlement` request | Authorised application/service/Authorisation adapter; beneficiary, capability/feature, requested quantity, scope, as-of time, required freshness | Returns permit/deny/indeterminate as *commercial eligibility only*; absence or stale proof never grants protected access |
| Governance/configuration ports | Identity tenant/scope facts, Authentication assurance, Authorisation decision, Configuration-approved limits, Legal Compliance applicability, Privacy constraints and Clock | Mandatory unknown/withdrawn authority blocks protected change; no inherited child-tenant access |
| Infrastructure ports | Host persistence, durable outbox/transport, Scheduler, optional Workflow, Notifications, provider adapters | No private-table imports, hidden transports or provider SDK leakage; mandatory durability fails closed |

**Information supplied by neighbours:** Pricing supplies attributable tariff/quote and effective revision (not term); Billing supplies assessed charge and status reference (not entitlement); Payment Gateway supplies verified capture/refund/reversal outcome (not settlement or access); Identity supplies opaque tenant/group/subject references (not right to a plan); Membership supplies separately qualified scheme facts (not subscription state); resource-owning services supply usage, quota consumption and provisioning evidence (not plan authority). Legal Compliance/Privacy constrain activation, renewal and retention; Configuration supplies approved settings but cannot redefine contractual invariants.

## Security, assurance, privacy and legal policy

Enforce per-resource server-side authorisation for every read/write and tenant-boundary decision, with phishing-resistant step-up where warranted for contract, payout-reference or cancellation-right changes. Follow OWASP ASVS 5 Level 2 (applicable Level 3 for sensitive changes), UK GDPR/GDPR purpose limitation, data minimisation, erasure/retention restrictions and lawful preservation under Legal Compliance. Legal rights to cancellation, trial conversion, digital-service renewal, price-change notice, consumer withdrawal, tax and sector-specific obligations depend on jurisdiction, counterparties and effective law; do not claim universal legal permission or automatic compliance. Preserve legal hold and defensible historical contract evidence with scoped retention.

A threat model should cover fraudulent subscription changes; entitlement escalation; cross-tenant leakage; replay/out-of-order events; double renewal/charge; stale pricing; forged payment confirmation; unverified consent/authority; time-zone/DST boundaries; provider outages; hostile plan/feature identifiers; and privilege confusion between commercial eligibility and IAM. Protect evidence at rest/in transit, redact financial and personal details from events/logs, audit all consequential changes and permit controlled corrections without erasing provenance. Domain error codes and safe `messageKey` come from Subscription; shared `error-normalisation` standardises transport without creating business decisions. Logging is for diagnostics, Audit for independently retained accountability and Reporting for derived analytics.

**Policy separation:** mandatory legal/security floors override contractual configurations; approved domain rules distinguish trial/grace, renewal timing, allocation and suspension; personal preferences affect notices/presentation only. Configuration holds approved settings and evidence; Subscription enforces its own terms. Renewal/cancellation policy must record jurisdiction, consumer/business classification, notice rules, contract version, consent and lawful effective dates, with revalidation on changes; show the impact before acceptance and preserve proof of notices sent/received when legally relevant. Do not assume an email was received or a legal right waived. The Legal Compliance capability supplies legally qualified applicable constraints; consumer renewal/withdrawal rules require jurisdiction-specific review before activation. Publisher may render a contract/notice but never makes it executable by publication alone. (Owner-approved SUB-14.)

|---|---|---|---|
| **Error Normalisation — stateless library** | Source-owned stable subscription error definitions and version, known/unknown command outcome, approved mapping, trusted correlation and disclosure policy | Safe category/code, `messageKey`, optional field/recovery hint, separate public and diagnostic projections, *advisory* retry qualification or explicit unclassified result | Pure transformation only. | **Logging — operational diagnostics** | Separately submits sanitised technical failure/latency codes, subscription operation type, severity, tenant context from trusted host, opaque correlation, reconciliation/queue/provider health; no sensitive terms, credentials or raw customer/payment data | Accepted/rejected/throttled/unavailable/uncertain intake receipt; authorised diagnostic queries, sink/backlog/drop and freshness health | Logging owns diagnostics and delivery status, **not** Subscription state, audited proof or guaranteed sink receipt. | **Audit — attributable evidence** | Independently selected risk-sensitive accepted/denied/attempted changes: plan publication, term consent, entitlement allocations, privileged overrides, renewals, cancellations, provider authority/cutover and security denials. | **Reporting — derived analytics** | Authorised, paginated, versioned plan/agreement/status/entitlement/history projections and lifecycle events, tenant/beneficiary scope, source revision, as-of/observed time, corrections, uncertainty and coverage indicators | Authorised subscription counts, active/trial/grace/churn/renewal trends, allocation/use comparisons from the correct owners, report execution and export status, source lineage, freshness, completeness and omissions | Reporting owns report definitions and derived snapshots, not subscription, Billing, Invoice or Accounting facts. Events trigger refresh; authoritative source queries prevent duplicate counting. Reporting outage or stale reports never block lifecycle operations or grant entitlements. |

**Shared enforcement:** treat diagnostic, evidence and analytical deliveries as **separate public calls or event consumers** rather than automatic dispatch by the normaliser. Enforce resource/tenant authorisation at source and recipient, minimal purpose-bound disclosure, revision and provenance, deduplication/reconciliation, accurate `pending/unknown` outcomes, and retention/legal holds. The host composes acyclic contract/port relationships; do not import private schemas.

**Contract acceptance:** (1) invalid plan produces safe normalised code with no technical-data leak; (2) a renewal timeout stays `unknown` until source read-back irrespective of retry hint; (3) Logging outage does not turn a valid business commit into failure; (4) an Audit-critical contract change cannot falsely finalise without its selected evidence guarantee; (5) a replayed event is not counted twice; (6) stale/unauthorised reports cannot reveal or alter entitlements; (7) all four neighbours operate without direct dependency cycles or transitive data leakage.

Pricing owns tariffs; Identity owns people; services own usage |
| **Restricted operator worklist** `/subscriptions/operations` | `getHistory`, `reconcileExternal`, operation status and source read-back | Investigate provider drift, migrations, uncertain payments, failed notices and critical Audit evidence; never trigger blind repeat collection |
| **Contextual indicators / Reporting** (host-chosen) | `evaluateEntitlement`, plan/status queries; authorised Reporting reports | Read-only plan/eligibility hints; Reporting owns derived trends. UI display never grants protected access or overrides authoritative results |

**Delivery choice:** The essential first-release UI comprises the subscriber dashboard, plan/terms flow, agreement detail/history, change/cancel confirmation and clear pending/rejected/unknown states. Plan authoring, advanced allocation, Reporting links and provider migration console are conditional extensions. Tenant administrators and restricted operators must be separated by server-enforced resource permissions and appropriate assurance; a hidden menu item is not an access control. A public customer host may mount the subscriber journeys outside the admin sidebar.

**Presentation rules:** Theme Manager semantic tokens and shared UI primitives; WCAG 2.2 AA (keyboard, visible focus, screen-reader state changes, accessible confirmations and cancellation without dark patterns); BCP 47, Clock/Intl dates, locale-aware currency; responsive layout; explicit loading/empty/denied/pending/unknown/error and recovery states. Never equate a redirect, charge estimate, invoice or payment callback with committed activation. Show provider and Audit uncertainty, legal notices and effective times truthfully; hold only minimal approved client state. Any browser storage must be recorded in `STORAGE-REGISTER.md`. Server-side queries, mutations and exports enforce independent tenant/resource authorisation, retention, redaction and legal restrictions.

**Headless mode:** No route, browser storage, presentation, Reporting or Theme Manager runtime requirement. The same published service contract must support non-Nuxt consumers. Future root `CONTRACT.md` must document optional public UI contributions, host configuration/route mounting, feature/permission checks and UI-disabled behaviour.
