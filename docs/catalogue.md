## Governing rules

1. Each capability owns a cohesive responsibility, classified as Foundation, Platform or Domain, independently of framework or deployment (ADR-0001). Nuxt capability layers retain headless cores and optional presentation under [ADR-0009](decisions/ADR-0009-headless-capability-layers.md).
2. Applications are thin composition roots, supplying infrastructure, configuration, routing and deployment choices. Framework-independent capabilities use documented independent consumers; Nuxt adapters retain ADR-0012's host budget ([ADR-0001](decisions/ADR-0001-capability-repositories-and-composition-shells.md), [ADR-0012](decisions/ADR-0012-trivial-composition.md)).
3. Contract dependencies and direct Nuxt extension edges are explicitly declared, directed and jointly acyclic. Service use is through public contracts and host-supplied ports; deliberate schema-validated archetype specialisation and Nuxt extension of documented public contributions are permitted under [ADR-0016](decisions/ADR-0016-controlled-layer-specialisation-and-composition.md). No extension implies access to private source.
4. Stateful capabilities own their persistence ports, schemas, migrations and any other stores ([ADR-0002](decisions/ADR-0002-composition-supplied-persistence-and-capability-owned-schemas.md), [ADR-0006](decisions/ADR-0006-polyglot-persistence-and-data-store-security.md)). No shared persistence service is permitted, and required persistence fails closed when its provider is absent.
5. Identity owns users, groups, the single-parent group hierarchy and memberships; Profile owns personal data; Authentication owns sessions; Authorisation owns access decisions. Every human identity has one system-managed personal group.
6. Every protected operation is authorised on the server. Security follows OWASP ASVS 5.0 Level 2, with applicable Level 3 controls for security-sensitive operations.
7. Presentation meets WCAG 2.2 AA, supports internationalisation and uses Theme Manager's semantic vocabulary. Privacy, data minimisation, retention and consent apply across capabilities.
8. Separate deployment is optional; the default architecture is a modular monolith. Capability ownership, implementation and deployment are distinct; refer to the [Capability Standard](standards/capability-standard.md).

### Reviews and external reputation providers

`reviews` distinguishes verified purchase, verified service/experience, invited and unverified submissions. Verification requires evidence from the authoritative ordering, booking or equivalent source, not a self-asserted flag. Protect reviewer privacy, moderation auditability, business responses and transparent provenance. Reviews and reputational aggregation do not, by themselves, implement personalised product recommendations; a separate recommendation-algorithm boundary must be justified if needed.

The optional **Trustpilot adapter** belongs under `integrations` (conceptually `integrations/trustpilot`), not as a required `reviews` dependency or automatically as its own Nuxt Layer. It translates permitted invitations, reviews, ratings and replies through documented external APIs and retains source attribution and verification meaning. The reviews core must function without it; external provider terms, credentials and data transfers require independent approval.

### Organisational archetypes versus business domains

An organisational archetype MAY describe metadata schemas, positions, requested role profiles, typed reporting relationships and capability configuration. It MUST NOT become a second Identity group hierarchy, directly grant access, assert asset permissions, or absorb independent domain lifecycles. Permission profiles are requests interpreted under existing Authorisation and domain policies. Reporting relationships are not access-control inheritance.

`organisation`, `business`, `company`, `club`, `committee`, `department` and `office` are **organisational archetypes or application compositions by default**, not seven independent capability candidates. They describe a group's purpose, type, configuration or presentation through published contracts; they never duplicate Identity's groups, membership or hierarchy. A separate domain is justified only by demonstrably independent durable records, governance rules and lifecycle, such as legal-entity filings, formal committee decisions or facility management. Such domain ownership requires its own review rather than following automatically from an organisational label.

### Relationship vocabulary

| Relationship | Meaning | Example (illustrative, not a declared dependency) |
|---|---|---|
| Requires | Essential public service/port contract; absence fails closed | Warehouse may require Inventory operations |
| Optionally uses | Optional published contract and documented absence mode | Reviews may integrate external reputation providers |
| Specialises | Validated archetype, configuration or documented extension surface | Greengrocer specialises a retail archetype |
| Extends (Nuxt) | Direct, documented Nuxt framework inheritance with merge/override rules | A retail app extends a presentation/composition shell |
| Composes | Application/integration assembly of independent capability contracts | Shop application combines Product, Pricing and Order |

These are distinct edges: `requires` is not synonymous with Nuxt `extends`, and a business event is not an inheritance mechanism. Dependency cycles are prohibited over their combined graph where edges denote runtime/build dependencies (ADR-0001 and ADR-0016).

## Consolidations and aliases

No shared persistence service (ADR-0002); user/group services belong to Identity and sessions to Authentication. Image/media-library concepts belong to Media; storage-service to Storage; policy-map to Legal Compliance; integration-adapters to Integrations; doc-search to Search; background job/scheduling services to Scheduler; workflow-service to Workflow; notification-service to Notifications; event-bus-service/events-queue to Message Queue. Blog and workshop are Publisher/Event/Course specialisations unless a separate domain is justified. Document must not duplicate Publisher. Tenancy belongs to Identity; tenant administration may be presentation. The former todo-manager concept is superseded by Todo; application-compositions is historical vocabulary. Retain UUID-generator as a legacy published identifier for uuid-generator. Existing published repository names and contracts require independently approved migrations.

## Starting a capability

A repository is created only once these are established:

1. purpose and bounded responsibility;
2. owned state, rules and lifecycle;
3. explicit exclusions;
4. provided public contracts and any schema versions required by tooling;
5. required and optional capability contracts;
6. configuration and persistence-provider requirements;
7. security, privacy, accessibility and internationalisation obligations;
8. independent contract, integration and consumer acceptance tests.

The group model is defined in [Identity and Access](standards/identity-and-access-standard.md) ([ADR-0003](decisions/ADR-0003-group-model-and-identity-first.md)).

## Tenant governance, policy authority and commercial onboarding (ADR-0017/ADR-0018)

The Identity-owned **tenant hierarchy** is distinct from its accepted single-parent **group hierarchy**. Tenant parentage allows only specifically authorised, scoped administration and never transfers access to child private data, Identity membership, commercial billing responsibility, group ownership or domain source authority. Active tenants require verified accountable human digital governance; creator provenance, real-world controlling legal entity, director/shareholder, delegated administrator and payer remain distinct. Protected transfer, recovery and portability follow [ADR-0017](decisions/ADR-0017-tenant-hierarchy-and-accountable-digital-control.md). These are **new target contract obligations**, not claims about current Identity APIs.

Policy categories remain separate: immutable architectural/security constraints (enforced by responsible owners), scoped Configuration values, organisational governance rules, domain business rules, qualified Legal Compliance and Privacy constraints, authoritative governing instruments, and Publisher-managed policy documents. No generic `policies` service is created; published statements do not constitute executable controls or legal proof. See [ADR-0018](decisions/ADR-0018-policy-governance-and-commercial-entitlements.md).

The host's operating company is an ordinary scoped commercial tenant/business party; the installation is not itself a tenant. Modular paid tenant onboarding composes Product/Pricing, Order, Payment Gateway, Subscription, Billing, Invoice, Accounting, Identity, Configuration, Authorisation, Workflow and relevant resource owners via public boundaries. **Entitlement, provisioning, enablement and individual permission are independent.** Logical purchased tenant image/file storage quota must be demonstrably enforced before advertised; Storage's existing usage report alone does not enforce a quota. Bill-to party and consuming tenant may differ with proper authority. `business`, `organisation`, `club`, `company` and `committee` remain archetypes/compositions absent independent domain authority; no new `tenancy` capability is inferred by tenant-tree requirements.

## Adopted conceptual allocations

The owner-approved conceptual allocations are adopted with this consolidation, subject to accepted ADRs and without implying implementation acceptance. New Domain responsibilities: party (scoped legal/commercial references, not Identity/Profile/Verification); payroll (pay calculations and qualified filing/disbursement evidence, not Accounting settlement or Employment terms); workforce-scheduling (planned shifts/rosters, not actual time or technical Scheduler). Accounting owns approved financial budgets and allocations; Employment owns establishment capacity and actual occupancy; Order owns purchase/sale framework terms; other general agreements retain qualified external authority. Attendance owns generic participation/presence; Training educational determination. Workflow cannot bypass mandatory approvals. No general Contracts, Finance, Procurement or Usage capability.

Localisation replaces the empty translate candidate without a published alias. Translation is one function; internationalisation remains cross-cutting. Source owners retain content and approval; Configuration/Profile retain preferences and Clock retains temporal semantics. External providers are replaceable; legal and safety-critical translation requires suitable source-owner review.
